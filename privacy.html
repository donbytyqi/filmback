Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Filmback does not collect any data about you.

What stays on your phone

  • Camera. Filmback uses the camera only to photograph your film while you scan. The photos are processed on your iPhone and are never uploaded.
  • Photos. Filmback saves finished scans to Photos when you export, and reads photos only when you choose to import scans you already have.
  • Your scans and settings are stored on your iPhone, inside the app. Deleting the app deletes them.

Filmback has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It works fully offline.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is handled by Apple through the App Store. Filmback never sees your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to purchases.

Sharing a scan with the developer

If you choose to send a scan or a capture to the developer, for example to report a problem, you send it yourself, through the share sheet or email app you pick. It is used only to look into that problem.

Children

Filmback does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
